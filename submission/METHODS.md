# comp5 Track 3 — KRAS G12D pMHC binders: methods and evidence

**20 de novo single-chain protein minibinders, 69–95 aa, all within the published
40–100 aa molecule category 1.** `molecule_class = protein`. Rows are ranked, top row first.

## Surface targeted

**The peptide itself and the groove immediately around it** — specifically the solvent-exposed
central bulge of `VVVGADGVGK` at positions **p4, p5, p7, p8**, with p6 and p9 tracked in contact
accounting but not targeted.

Chosen from measurement, not assumption. Per-residue SASA on 7OW6 with the TCR removed:

| pos | aa | SASA Å² | rel. exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| p4 | G | 37.0 | 58.3% |
| p5 | A | 26.9 | 27.2% |
| p6 | D | 21.8 | 18.5% |
| p7 | G | **75.5** | **83.8%** |
| p8 | V | 46.6 | 38.1% |
| p9 | G | 15.7 | 28.0% |
| p1 / p10 | V / K | 1.2 / 6.7 | 0.6% / 2.6% (anchors, excluded) |

**The mutant Asp at p6 is not read directly, and no design claims to.** Its side chain is 10.4%
exposed and locked in a bidentate salt bridge to HLA Arg114 (OD1–NH1 3.04 Å, OD2–NH2 2.95 Å)
plus an H-bond to Gln70 (OD2–NE2 3.08 Å). Specificity here can only be indirect, through altered
surface electrostatics and bulge conformation, exactly as the reference TCR work describes.

The whole peptide presents only **239.4 Å²**. That ceiling matters: an area-based
peptide-contact fraction cannot exceed ~0.20 at a Baker-reference 1,200 Å² interface, so
absolute buried peptide area is reported instead of a fraction.

## Pipeline

RFdiffusion (hotspot-conditioned on chain C, α1/α2 platform crop) → ProteinMPNN / SolubleMPNN,
4 labelled pools → **AF2-initial-guess** → **Boltz-2 three-state** (g12d / wt / empty).

**AF2-initial-guess is the selection filter.** All 20 clear the canonical published criteria:
`pae_interaction < 10`, `plddt_binder > 80`, `target_aligned_rmsd < 5 Å`. Measured pae range
**3.84–9.70**. This filter passed 158 of 11,152 screened designs (1.4%), and was validated
against 32 composition-matched scrambled sequences, **0 of which reached pae < 10** (best
scramble 19.56).

**Boltz-2 three-state supplies the objective-1 and objective-2 differentials**, at **5 averaged
diffusion samples**. This is not optional: at one sample `iptm_margin_wt` has a between-seed
Spearman ρ of **+0.239** — the ranking is near-random under a reseed. At five it is **+0.846**.

## Honest limits

- **Ranking by `iptm_margin_wt` is meaningful only for the top few rows.** The 5-sample noise
  floor is 0.053 (mean |Δ| between seeds). Ranks 1–2 (0.178, 0.084) clear it; ranks ~5–20
  (0.009–0.033) do **not**. Below the top handful the ordering on objective 1 is not resolved
  by this metric, and we do not claim it is. All 20 are positive in direction.
- **`iptm_margin_empty` is reproducible (ρ +0.881) but did not discriminate** peptide-targeted
  designs from deliberately-built β2m framework decoys (+0.034 separation). It is reported, not
  relied on, as evidence of peptide dependency. Buried peptide area does discriminate: 68.3 Å²
  median for peptide-targeted designs vs 0.0 Å² for framework decoys.
- **Not computed:** Rosetta ddG, PRODIGY, ThermoMPNN, APBS electrostatics — none installed.
  Recorded as `not_run` throughout rather than estimated.
- **No Rosetta FastRelax** between sequence design and AF2, which upstream `dl_binder_design`
  performs. A real deviation.
- **Nothing here is a measurement.** All values are predicted/modelled. No K_D is claimed, and
  Boltz-2's affinity head was not used.

## De novo provenance

Backbones were generated by RFdiffusion from the target structure alone and sequences designed
onto them by ProteinMPNN. **No known binder sequence entered the pipeline at any point** — no
TCR, TCR-mimic, or published design was used as seed, template, or graft. Verified after the
fact against the JDI TCR lineage (7OW6 chains D/E): maximum 20-residue window identity **0.400**
against a 0.70 threshold, 0 of 60 flagged.

## Diversity

20 designs over **13 distinct backbones**, from 4 independent generation arms (deep resampling
12, long 4, fresh 3, β-topology 1), including non-helical topology from the β arm.
